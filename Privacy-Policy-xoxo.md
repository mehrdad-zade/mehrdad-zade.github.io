## Overview

Tic Tac Toe – XOXO is a simple two-player and single-player puzzle game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects — and why it collects none.

---

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs are included.
- No advertising networks are integrated.
- No data is sent to any server — the app runs entirely on your device.

---

## Data Stored on Device

All game state (scores, current game) exists only in memory and is discarded when the app is closed. Nothing is written to disk beyond what iOS manages automatically (e.g., standard app lifecycle state).

---

## Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services, frameworks, or SDKs that collect data.

---

## Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.
