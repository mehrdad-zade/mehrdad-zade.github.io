# Privacy Policy — Binge Quest

**Last updated:** July 2026

## Summary

Binge Quest does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

## Data Collection

Binge Quest does **not** collect:

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Health data transmitted to any server
- Usage analytics or crash reports
- Device identifiers
- Any other data

## Data Storage

All data you enter (age, height, weight, binge logs, notification preferences) is stored exclusively in:

1. **SwiftData on your device** — the app's local database, stored inside the app's shared container on your iPhone
2. **App Group shared container** — used only for communication between the main app, widget, and notification extension running on the same device

No data is ever written to iCloud, synced to any server, or accessible by any party other than you.

## Third-Party Services

Binge Quest uses **no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external services**. The app uses only Apple's first-party frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, WidgetKit, UserNotifications).

## Notifications

Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Apple's `UserNotifications` framework. No push notification server is used. Notification content is generated locally and never transmitted anywhere.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time from within the app: **Settings → Delete All Data**. Deleting the app from your device removes all associated data permanently.

## Children

Binge Quest is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in app updates.
