# Privacy Policy for Gist Reader

**Effective date: April 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Gist Reader ("the app") handles your information.

---

## Information We Collect

### Device & App Identifiers
The app uses Firebase, which automatically generates an anonymous installation ID to identify your device session. This is required for the app to function. No personal account is created.

### Search Queries
When you search for books, movies, or TV shows, your search terms are sent to third-party catalog APIs to retrieve results.

### App Interactions & Installed Apps
The app displays ads via Google AdMob, which automatically collects app interaction data and installed apps information for ad targeting purposes.

### Diagnostics
Firebase App Check collects device integrity data to verify the app is legitimate and protect backend services from abuse.

---

## How We Use Your Information

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Anonymous device ID | App functionality, Firestore access, security verification |
| Search queries | Retrieve book/movie/TV search results |
| App interactions & installed apps | Ad targeting and delivery |
| Device integrity data | Fraud prevention and security |

---

## Third Parties We Share Data With

| Third Party | Data Shared | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase / Firestore | Anonymous device ID | App functionality, security |
| Google AdMob | Device ID, app interactions, installed apps | Advertising |
| Anthropic | Book/movie title, author, genre | AI summary generation |
| OpenAI | Book/movie title, author, genre | AI summary generation (fallback) |
| Google Books API | Search queries | Book search |
| OMDb API | Search queries | Movie search |
| TVmaze | Search queries | TV show search |
| iTunes API | Search queries | Media search |
| Open Library | Search queries | Book search |

---

## Data Security

All data transmitted by the app uses HTTPS encryption in transit. No cleartext HTTP connections are used.

---

## User Accounts

Gist Reader does not require or support user account creation. The app uses anonymous authentication only.

---

## Data Retention & Deletion

Your local library data is stored on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app. Anonymous identifiers managed by Firebase and Google AdMob are subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
